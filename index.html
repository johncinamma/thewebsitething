01 / MISSIONThe Charge

To fly, fight, and win in the dark.

The 160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment provides dedicated aviation support to Army special operations forces and other government agencies. Its mission is to deliver special operations forces to and from objective areas under cover of darkness, in any environment, anywhere on earth.

Night Stalkers train relentlessly to master no-notice deployment, night flight, and sustained operations — often flying in blackout conditions, nap-of-the-earth, relying solely on sensors and instinct. When the nation calls, the 160th answers.

04 / LINEAGEReal Life SOAR Regimental History

Forged in Adversity

From a failed rescue mission in the Iranian desert to the unit that delivered SEALs to Abbottabad — four decades of courage, sacrifice, and excellence in the dark.

1980

Operation Eagle Claw Aftermath

Following the failed hostage rescue mission in Iran, the U.S. Army identified a critical gap: dedicated, specially trained aviation assets for special operations.

1981

Task Force 158 Formed

Colonel Jerry Boykin establishes Task Force 158 at Fort Campbell, Kentucky, drawing volunteers from the 101st Aviation Battalion to forge a new breed of night-flying aviator.

1986

Redesignated 160th SOAR

The unit is officially activated as the 160th Aviation Battalion (Special Operations), later redesignated the 160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment.

1990

Operation Desert Storm

Night Stalkers execute a battalion-sized air assault to launch the ground war, inserting special forces deep behind Iraqi lines under total darkness.

1993

Battle of Mogadishu

The regiment supports the longest sustained firefight since Vietnam. Their actions are later chronicled in Black Hawk Down — a defining moment in the unit's legacy.

2001

War on Terror Begins

Within weeks of 9/11, Night Stalkers insert SOF into Afghanistan, beginning two decades of continuous combat operations in the most demanding environments.

2011

Operation Neptune Spear

Modified MH-60 helicopters flown by 160th aviators deliver Navy SEALs to the compound in Abbottabad, Pakistan, for the mission that eliminated Osama bin Laden.

Today

Constant Vigilance

The regiment remains the world's premier rotary-wing special operations force, training continuously to fly, fight, and win in any condition, any terrain, any hour.

06 / ACCESSIONBecoming a Night Stalker

Join the Regiment

The BRM5 160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment offers the most elite and highest calibre of training and event sessions daily. If you are interested in learning or conducting milsim, we need you!

01

Assessment & Selection

A 3-week assessment of flying skills, physical fitness, and mental resilience. Only the most qualified candidates are selected.

02

Green Platoon (SOAR Qualification)

A grueling 6-month course covering night vision goggle flight, aerial gunnery, and special operations tactics.

03

Regimental Assignment

Graduates earn the Night Stalker tab and are assigned to one of the three battalions at Fort Campbell, KY.

04

Continuous Training

Night Stalkers never stop training — maintaining readiness for no-notice deployment anywhere in the world.
